Personal creative project

Personal Instagram

A low-stakes space to test ideas.

Instagram

I work from home and spend most of my day creating within a B2B brand environment, so my personal Instagram gives me another creative outlet. When an idea or format does not fit the company channels I manage, I can experiment with it here instead.

I post when I have something I want to make rather than following a strict schedule. The content is mostly lifestyle-led: everyday life, useful information, travel and funny talking-to-camera videos.

It also gives me a practical way to stay close to social trends, test different hooks, edits and formats, and learn how content behaves without using a company account as the testing ground.
